# 🌍 VYOMNETRA AI

### Predicting What Happens Next

VYOMNETRA AI is an AI-powered Earth Intelligence Platform designed to transform disaster response from reactive to proactive.

Unlike traditional systems that only detect disasters, VYOMNETRA AI introduces **Project ECHO**, an intelligent prediction engine that analyzes disaster impacts and forecasts their cascading consequences on infrastructure, healthcare access, transportation networks, and vulnerable communities.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

During natural disasters, authorities often know where the damage occurred but struggle to understand:

- Which villages may become isolated
- Which hospitals could lose accessibility
- Which supply routes may fail
- Which communities will require urgent intervention

Current systems primarily focus on detection rather than impact prediction.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

VYOMNETRA AI combines:

- Satellite Imagery
- Geospatial Intelligence
- Infrastructure Analysis
- Predictive Risk Modeling

to provide actionable disaster intelligence.

The platform helps decision-makers understand not only where a disaster occurred, but also what could happen next.

---

## ⭐ Project ECHO

Project ECHO is the core intelligence engine of VYOMNETRA AI.

Example:

Cyclone
↓
Bridge Damaged
↓
Road Blocked
↓
Village Isolated
↓
Medicine Delayed
↓
Population At Risk
↓
Emergency Priority Alert

Project ECHO predicts the ripple effects of disasters before secondary impacts become critical emergencies.
